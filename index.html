cherry's labs

Cherry's Laboratories is a small lab studio run by a collection of nerds with a very diverse set of skills. We aim to create custom solutions for clients, research and development, and build out our ideas. The goal is collaborative learning and production, combining our skill sets into useful tools, software, hardware, and anything else. (more at cherryslabs.com).

Informally, it's a design-and-dev studio — we take on client branding, build up their business presence, and build our own projects for fun, including a couple of the things listed above.

My side of it is mostly design and ideas: brand identity, visual direction, and the R&D thinking behind what we build next.
